About Africa: Past & Present

Africa: Past & Present is an educational blog dedicated to exploring the history, heritage and development of the African continent.

The blog connects Africa’s historical experiences with issues and opportunities in the modern world. It looks at ancient kingdoms and empires, trade and natural resources, culture, governance, economic development, science and technology.

Our purpose

Our purpose is to make African history and contemporary issues easier to understand through informative articles, maps, examples and reliable sources. We believe that learning about the past can help readers better understand the present and imagine a stronger future.

What you will find here

  • Ancient African kingdoms and empires.
  • Trade routes, natural resources and economic history.
  • African culture, heritage and influential people.
  • Politics, governance and social development.
  • Science, technology and Africa’s future.

Thank you for visiting. Explore, learn and join us in discovering the many stories of Africa.
